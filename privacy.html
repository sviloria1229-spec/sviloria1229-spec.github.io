Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Studio Reels TikTok is a personal automation tool operated by Samuel Viloria (the "Owner"). Contact: Sviloria1229@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app is used only by the Owner to upload the Owner's own videos and captions to the Owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. It has no other users.

Data we handle

  • TikTok authorization tokens for the Owner's account, used only to publish the Owner's own content.
  • Video files and captions created by the Owner.

This data is stored only in the Owner's private accounts (the automation platform n8n and Google Drive and Google Sheets). We do not collect personal data from any other person.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and files are kept only as long as the Owner uses the tool. The Owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings and delete the stored files and tokens.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
